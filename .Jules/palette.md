## 2024-05-19 - Interactive Elements and Accessibility in Navigation
**Learning:** Icon-only anchor tags (like social media links) often lack accessible names when not utilizing text content, and their focus states might not contrast well against complex, animated backgrounds (like the 3D scene in this app). Additionally, mobile menu toggles implemented with generic elements need explicit ARIA ties (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) to inform screen readers of their purpose and state.
**Action:** When adding interactive icons or building custom toggles (like mobile menus), always include `aria-label`s for icons and use `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls` for disclosure widgets. Furthermore, utilize Tailwind's `focus-visible` utilities (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-sky-500`) to guarantee that keyboard focus is visually distinct and prominent, even against dark or dynamic backgrounds.
